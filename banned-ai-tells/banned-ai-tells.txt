# banned-ai-tells.txt  (machine list for check_tells.py)
# Source: @Zephyr_hg, https://x.com/Zephyr_hg/status/2116643887624430055 (2026-10-31 2:30 PM PT)
# Format: one Python regex per line, matched case-insensitively. Blank lines and lines starting
# with "# " are comments. A line "#@ <id> | <label> | <fix>" starts a category; patterns under it
# report that category. (?-i:...) forces case-sensitive for that part (used for BREAKING).
# The nine categories are Zephyr's, in his order. Patterns are derived from his examples only.
# Category 0 is the existing fleet rule (the em dash the post builds on), not one of the nine.

#@ 0 | Em or en dash (existing fleet rule) | use a comma, period or parentheses
\u2014
\u2013

#@ 1 | "Quietly." | delete the word. If it shipped, it shipped
\bquietly\b

#@ 2 | "It's not X, it's Y." | say what it does
\b(it|this|that)(['\u2019]s| is)\s+not\s+(just\s+|only\s+)?[^.!?\n]{1,60}?[,;:]\s*(it|this|that)(['\u2019]s| is)\b
\b(it|this|that)(['\u2019]s| is)\s+not\s+(just\s+|only\s+)?[^.!?\n]{1,60}?\.\s+(it|this|that)(['\u2019]s| is)\b
\bit(['\u2019]s| is)n['\u2019]?t\s+(just\s+)?[^.!?\n]{1,60}?[,;.]\s*(it|this|that)(['\u2019]s| is)\b

#@ 3 | The fake deadline | say how long the setup takes. Leave the reader's calendar alone
\bbefore (monday|tuesday|wednesday|thursday|friday|saturday|sunday|the weekend)\b
\b(do|set|build|try|start|ship|finish|get)\b[^.!?\n]{0,30}\bthis weekend\b

#@ 4 | Talking to a crowd ("most people...") | talk to the one person reading. Say what you do instead
\bmost people\b

#@ 5 | "Can now X like a pro." | show the output and let them judge the price
\bcan now \w+ like an?\s
\blike a pro\b

#@ 6 | The little line that sounds deep | delete it. The sentence before it already said it
\b(that|this|it)(['\u2019]s| is) the whole game\b
\bthe part (everyone|everybody|most people|nobody) skips\b

#@ 7 | Three of everything up top ("Write. Edit. Ship.") | keep the one reason you actually believe
(?:^|[.!?]\s+)[a-z]+\.\s+[a-z]+\.\s+[a-z]+\.(?=\s|$)

#@ 8 | Adjectives in a pile ("powerful, seamless, game-changing") | name the thing. If it's good, the name is enough
\bpowerful\b
\bseamless(ly)?\b
\bgame[- ]chang(ing|er)\b

#@ 9 | Sirens, arrows, CAPITALS | write the news as a normal sentence
\U0001F6A8
(?-i:\bBREAKING\b)
[\u2193\u2B07\u2935\U0001F447]

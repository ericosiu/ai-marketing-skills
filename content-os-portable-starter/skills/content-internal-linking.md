# Content Internal Linking — Portable Mirror

Use `plan` mode before drafting and `audit-repair` on final copy.

1. Build a live URL universe from declared sitemap shards; deduplicate by canonical URL.
2. Open candidate destinations and verify status, final URL, title/H1, canonical, and topic match.
3. Check whether an existing page already owns the proposed primary intent.
4. Plan contextual links after the introduction; never guess paths or force irrelevant links.
5. On final bytes, verify destinations, anchors, claim match, self-link count, and external citations.
6. Bind the receipt to the final source hash. Any later copy edit invalidates the receipt.
7. Publishing and CMS writes remain separately approved.

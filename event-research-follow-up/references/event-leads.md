# Event lead collection and qualification

## Establish the cohort and evidence

Keep photo/conversation leads and the full attendee roster as separate cohorts. Many roster members are cold. Never imply they met the user, heard an offer or expressed interest without evidence. Preserve the event, source date, original source pointer and relationship status.

Use the user's gesture rubric for that event. Document what each observed gesture means for this event; it measures the user's defined priority, not company budget or purchase intent. Record the observed count, confidence and priority separately. User corrections override provisional classifications and must flow into every export and review page. Resolve people using readable badges and verified directory name/company text, not facial identification. Hold uncertain matches.

Photo order can suggest which offer was discussed, but it is not proof. Store an uncertain recruiting-versus-marketing assignment as tentative until supported by notes or user confirmation. Do not invent a precise session history from timestamps alone.

Give each person a stable ID and group by operating company. Preserve aliases and diacritics. Deduplicate across photos, directory entries and prior outreach; a surname alone is not a match.

## Collect efficiently

Check early for an authorized export, supported web directory or CSV, existing cached data and sufficient connector tools. Prefer structured text or visible DOM/accessibility extraction over one screenshot at a time. Honor the user's chosen app when they prefer it; do not repeatedly divert them into a web login.

In attendee apps, email and mobile often appear near the bottom. Let the profile load, inspect the full profile and scroll to its actual bottom before marking a field missing. Missing email does not imply missing phone. Save each completed record immediately.

A blank screen can be loading. A manual scroll or keystroke working does not prove automated input works. Inspect fresh state after a bounded wait, retry with fresh visible targets, and use one ordinary available fallback for the same source. Avoid both premature “stalled” reports and endless empty-page polling. UI element indices can change after each state refresh.

An approved text/image evaluator can batch review supplied text and snapshots. They cannot make the app load faster or extract a contact that has not been captured. Keep capture, visual classification, enrichment and outreach as separate stages. Use supported UI controls for native apps; do not inspect hidden app state, extract sessions or invent endpoints.

Measure a small representative pilot: profiles completed per minute, load/scroll time, retries and verification overhead. Estimate the remaining roster from observed throughput, with a range and separate export-versus-app scenarios. Model latency is not end-to-end collection speed. State a record/time checkpoint before a large pass.

## Enrich and qualify

Reuse existing matches before paid lookups. Apollo standard matching or another approved provider must match name plus company/domain and retain provenance. Keep provider credit limits and evaluator cost limits in separate cumulative ledgers, including retries. Prior event budgets are not future permission. Use the main skill's evaluation contract before new evaluator questions or image-based routing; uncertain model output stays review-only.

For email and phone, preserve source, collection date, verification method and confidence. Distinguish an address copied from the event app, a provider-verified address and an inference. Never guess an address or substitute a colleague, relative, namesake or former employer contact.

Company size is a required qualification field, not optional decoration. Record revenue and headcount ranges, evidence URLs, source date, period, currency, estimate status and operating entity. Unknown remains unknown. Fund assets, parent revenue and former employer figures do not establish the current company's budget. Size is a budget-fit signal, not proof of available spend.

Use the user's configured direct-client screen unless the user changes it. Smaller businesses may still be good referral or complementary partners. Select an evidence-based angle: customer test, marketing delivery partnership, referral, operating-company introduction or education. Talent firms can refer marketing leads; competitors need a specific complementary service. Do not infer portfolio access from someone's title.

When explicitly in scope, distinguish recruiting agents for AI-native talent from managed marketing agents for AEO, SEO, ad creative or cold outbound infrastructure. Record why an offer could help and what remains to verify. Do not manufacture a channel weakness to personalize a draft.

## Draft for the actual relationship

For warm conversation contacts, explain the relevant offer and ask whether it would be useful to them or someone they know. Invite another useful angle, including a marketing partnership. For cold attendees, use truthful shared event context without claiming a conversation or interest. Continue existing threads where appropriate; acknowledge referrals, promised replies and booked meetings instead of restarting a generic pitch.

Use the user's approved sender name, affiliation, signature, voice and verified public links. Do not inherit another person's identity, slogan or affiliations. Ask a concise question about whether the actual offer would be useful, including a relevant referral or complementary partnership only when supported.

Prepare day +5 and day +10 follow-up drafts when requested. Anchor dates to actual initial send, not draft creation or scheduled time. A later light monthly touch is a separate plan, not automatic enrollment. Recheck replies, declines, out-of-office dates and meetings before every follow-up; suppress or route to a personal response as appropriate.

A possible content angle is how AI turns event notes into qualified, personal follow-up. Content is a separate deliverable: use sanitized examples and measured time/cost results, and obtain permission before publishing identities, screenshots or messages. Do not claim pipeline or ROI from a sorted list or reply count.

## Opportunity table presentation

For opportunity and reply tables, include: person/company; what the company does in one plain-language sentence (product/service and customer); size with evidence quality; actual response or stated need; proposed offer/partnership angle; angle strength and a short reason; and next qualification step.

Rate angle strength separately from company size, gesture priority and reply warmth. Use Strong when a specific stated need maps directly to an offer; Moderate when there is a plausible fit or concrete introduction but the need remains unconfirmed; Weak when the connection is speculative or the ask mainly benefits the other party; Unknown when evidence is missing. State budget, buyer access or scope gaps even for a Strong angle. A large company or friendly reply alone does not establish a strong angle. Clearly label inferred angles versus confirmed interest.

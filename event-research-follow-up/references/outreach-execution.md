# Approved event outreach execution

## Authority and readiness

Establish the exact cohort, exclusions, copy/version, sender account, channels, timing, cost and stopping point. Prepare a reviewable result before requesting any missing approval. Existing explicit approval persists within its scope; do not ask again merely because a pass resumed. Newly added recipients or channels need coverage by the approval. A research task never implies sending, CRM enrollment or full-roster collection.

Verify the sender in each service. Do not substitute another connected email account. Verify LinkedIn identity and the phone/WhatsApp sender before action. An inaccessible account is a blocker for that channel, not permission to borrow credentials or switch identity.

Keep identity, address and timezone holds excluded until the underlying evidence is resolved. Do not let an enrichment match silently clear a manual hold. Read current channel logs and recent replies before acting.

## Schedule across time zones

Resolve “tomorrow” to an absolute date in the user's timezone and show recipient-local dates. Use IANA zones and daylight-saving rules. Base zones on supported home/business location; a chapter or phone country code alone is insufficient. Record confidence and retain conflicting locations as holds. Weekend sends are allowed when the user explicitly chooses them.

Use recipient-local 9am–6pm unless the approved plan differs. Stagger batches and calculate the intersection of local windows, actual email-send times, channel rate limits and available pass times before launch. Prioritize closing windows and lead priority. A few fixed sender-timezone passes may miss entire regions; report coverage gaps in advance rather than promise full completion.

Use a bounded pass count and active-work cap from the task. For phone notices, set a conservative task-specific rate within the service's rules and approved plan. Record exact action times; use a buffer if UI timestamps only show minutes. Do not create indefinite recurrence or silently add passes to finish overflow.

## Email: scheduled is not sent

Keep approved copy snapshots separate from scheduling and delivery state. Record the provider's draft/message/thread IDs and both UTC and local schedule. Verify scheduling by readback, then separately verify actual sent status, exact recipient, sender, subject/body and timestamp.

Never resend an initial email because a tool response is ambiguous or a draft disappeared. A closed draft may be discarded or in flight. Resolve against the source and existing logs before retrying. Scheduled/pending is not sent; sent is not delivered or read.

Before any phone notice claiming an email was sent, verify the actual sent message and check for replies/declines. Do not send a notice to someone who already replied as if they had not responded.

## Phone notice

Use the exact app phone, normalized only with clear country-code evidence. Prefer WhatsApp internationally; use SMS when available and appropriate, never both for the same notice. Check prior channel history and the duplicate ledger first.

Warm conversation notice template, only when true:

> Hi [first name], this is [approved sender name] from [verified affiliation]. I sent you an email following up on our conversation at [event]. Let me know if the offer would be useful or if you see a relevant partnership.

Cold attendees require different, approved copy without a claimed conversation. Match the notice to the actual offer sent.

Use supported computer-use controls for a native messaging app. Search the supported exact number, inspect the recipient/header and chat history, enter the draft, and read it back before sending. If simulated typing scrambles text, use the supported paste action and verify it. Do not send until recipient, sender and draft are correct.

After sending, verify the outgoing bubble and status. Native accessibility output can omit bubbles; use a screenshot receipt when needed. A sent checkmark is not read confirmation. If the result is ambiguous, record it and inspect before any retry. Failed SMS recipient entry or unavailable UI is an unsent action, not success and not permission to switch accounts.

## LinkedIn

Use saved provider profile links or focused visible searches. Verify name and current company text before connecting; namesakes stay on hold. Skip existing connections and pending invitations. Accepting a verified incoming invitation is appropriate when the approved scope is to connect with that person. A follow is not a connection.

Use an approved truthful note or an approved no-note invitation. Verify Pending after the request, or first-degree/Remove connection after acceptance. A click alone is not proof. If nothing changes, allow load, inspect fresh state and make a bounded retry; do not blindly repeat actions or bypass challenges. Leave unrelated conversations untouched.

## Close the pass

Write each action and receipt immediately using the state contract. Check fresh replies before subsequent touches. Stop declines; defer out-of-office cases; route interested replies, referrals and meetings to personal handling within the authorized scope. Do not automatically send day +5/+10 drafts just because initial sends were approved.

Report concrete completed counts, remaining holds, deferred windows and actionable access blockers. At the cap, preserve the remaining objective and exact next step. Resume only under an existing bounded authorization or a new request. Close only task-created browser tabs; preserve pre-existing tabs and tabs explicitly requested to remain open.

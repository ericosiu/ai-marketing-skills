# Packaging Rubric

Use this rubric with the saved channel profile before recommending a package. Use the audit section only during profile bootstrap or an explicit refresh.

## Identity bootstrap or refresh only

Capture evidence from roughly 20 recent long-form thumbnails:

| Signal | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Palette | Dominant dark/light systems and accent colors |
| Subject | Face frequency, crop, expression, placement |
| Typography | Font character, casing, typical core word count |
| Composition | Split, triangle, centered, text-free, or object-led patterns |
| Motifs | Product marks, arrows, charts, money, systems, transformations |
| Title relationship | Repetition, complement, reveal, or metaphor |
| Exceptions | High-performing or feed-breaking variants |

Do not claim performance causality from raw views. Account for upload age, topic, and possible YouTube A/B variants.

## Hook lanes

Create packages from different viewer questions:

- **Verdict:** What surprising conclusion did the creator reach?
- **Proof:** What specific result, number, or transformation makes the claim tangible?
- **Utility:** What decision will the viewer make better after watching?

Avoid three synonyms for the same idea. Avoid incomplete experiments, unsupported superlatives, and recent thumbnail copy that would make the new upload indistinguishable in the feed.

Treat the lanes as promise hypotheses, not art directions. Changing the background, crop, or prop while preserving the same viewer question does not create a distinct package.

## Simplicity gate

Every candidate must pass before scoring:

- Use no more than three major visual groups plus one short text block.
- Count a face/cutout as one group, the dominant logo or object as one, and the outcome/proof metaphor as one.
- Count a symmetrical comparison pair or one cohesive campaign mark as one grouped unit.
- Do not count the background, divider, glow, or restrained decoration as groups.
- Give each side of a split comparison one dominant signal, not a cluster of props.
- At 320x180, the viewer must identify one focal relationship within one second.

When over budget, remove or combine components. Never solve clutter by shrinking objects, adding tiny UI, or stacking badges, arrows, charts, and checkmarks.

## Semantic clarity gate

Every candidate must also pass before scoring:

- At feed size, describe the focal relationship in one short literal sentence.
- Every visible symbol must be recognizable to the intended viewer or anchored by clear copy.
- Brand accuracy is not viewer recognition; an unfamiliar exact-model mark may still need a short label.
- Compare equivalent entities: model versus model or company versus company. Do not substitute a parent-company mark for one side and exact-model campaign art for the other unless that mismatch is the premise.
- One metaphor is enough. If a viewer must decode both the brand marks and the outcome metaphor, simplify.
- A consecutive upload in the same comparison group needs a materially different viewer outcome or a scheduling warning.
- Convert comparative verbs into visible hierarchy. The claimed winner or easier option must remain obvious when the copy is hidden.
- Do not let a cleaner path, larger icon, brighter color, or central position accidentally promote the competitor.
- Avoid numeric thumbnail copy when the video covers an open-ended or larger set than the displayed examples.

Fail the candidate if two plausible viewers could give materially different explanations of what the image promises.

## Show-and-tell evidence gate

For build demonstrations and operator case studies:

- make the real artifact, result screen, dashboard, output, or physical prop the dominant object when it remains legible at feed size;
- crop or simplify real UI rather than shrinking an entire interface;
- use an abstract metaphor only when the real artifact cannot communicate the promise safely or legibly;
- keep achieved numerical proof distinct from estimates, tests, and planned outcomes;
- require the opening 30 seconds to show the same evidence relationship promised by the package.

## Scoring

Score each candidate from 0 to 10:

| Dimension | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Transcript fidelity | 20% |
| Channel identity fit | 20% |
| Hook specificity and stakes | 20% |
| Title-thumbnail complementarity | 15% |
| Mobile legibility | 15% |
| Performance evidence and differentiation from recent uploads | 10% |

Recommend only candidates scoring at least 8.0 overall with no dimension below 7. If none pass, revise the weakest high-impact dimension once.

## Title-thumbnail contract

- Put searchable entities and the complete premise in the title.
- Put the shortest emotional payoff, contrast, or metaphor in the thumbnail.
- Do not repeat the same sentence twice.
- Prefer one face, one dominant object, and one obvious relationship.
- Use text-free packaging when the visual relationship is already unmistakable.

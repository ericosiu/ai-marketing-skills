## AEO Strategy Notes

**What makes content citable by AI models:**
- Clear, structured definitions in the first 100 words
- Numbered/bulleted lists with specific data points
- Comparison tables with structured data
- FAQ sections with concise, direct answers
- Strong E-E-A-T signals (author expertise, original data, citations)
- Entity consistency across the site (consistent brand, topic, and author entities)

**Platform-specific patterns from singlegrain.com's citation data:**
- **Google AI Mode** (330 citations) — by far the strongest platform. Keep structured content and clear topic entities.
- **Perplexity** (198 citations) — favors review/comparison content and how-to guides. The Semrush audit page is a model.
- **Copilot** (91 citations) — mid-tier. Tends to cite authoritative list content.
- **Google AI Overviews** (84 citations) — structured definitions and list content perform best.
- **ChatGPT** (72 citations) — cites specific, data-rich content. SaaS examples and tool reviews do well.
- **Gemini** (21 citations) and **Grok** (17 citations) — weakest. Opportunity: add more structured data, schema markup, and clear entity definitions.

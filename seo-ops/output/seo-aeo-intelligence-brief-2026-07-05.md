### AEO Content Principles to Apply
1. **Lead with definitions** — AI models preferentially cite clear, structured definitions
2. **Use numbered lists and step-by-step formats** — highest citation rate for how-to content
3. **Add FAQ schema** — direct mapping to AI question-answer patterns
4. **Include comparison tables** — models cite structured comparisons for "best X" queries
5. **Publish original data/frameworks** — AI models prefer citing unique, authoritative sources

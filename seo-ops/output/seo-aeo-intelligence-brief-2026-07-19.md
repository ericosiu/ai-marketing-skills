## Scoring Methodology

**SEO Score (1–10):** Weighted combination of search volume (30%), current position proximity to top 3 (25%), keyword difficulty feasibility (20%), CPC/commercial value (15%), existing ranking momentum (10%).

**AEO Score (1–10):** Evaluated on: (1) Does the SERP already show AI Overview? (2) Is the query a question, definition, comparison, or list? (3) Would AI models likely generate an answer for this query? (4) Can SG provide a definitive, citable answer with unique data? (5) Is the topic referenced in AI training conversations?

**Combined Priority = SEO Score + AEO Score** (max 20). Ties broken by traffic potential.

## SECTION 5: AEO CONTENT STRUCTURE RECOMMENDATIONS

For all pages targeting AI citation, apply this template:

1. **Lead with a concise answer** (40-60 words) in the first paragraph — this is what AI models extract
2. **Add a "Key Takeaways" box** at the top with 3-5 bullet points
3. **Use definition-style formatting**: "X is [definition]" for definitional queries
4. **Include structured comparison tables** for "best/top/vs" queries
5. **Add FAQ schema** with 5-8 questions — AI models use these heavily
6. **Include original data/statistics** with clear attribution — AI prefers citing primary sources
7. **Use step-by-step numbered lists** for process/how-to content
8. **Maintain strong E-E-A-T signals**: author bio, credentials, "reviewed by" badges

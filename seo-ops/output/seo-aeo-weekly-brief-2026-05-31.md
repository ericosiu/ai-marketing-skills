## AI/AEO Landscape Insights (Week of 2026-05-31)

### Key Signals from This Week

1. **Google Marketing Live 2026:** Gemini is now the OS underneath all Google ad products. New "Conversational Discovery" ads and "Highlighted Answers" embed ads into AI Mode — this changes the SEO/AEO equation fundamentally.

2. **ChatGPT Ads Manager goes self-serve:** ~50M daily shopping queries in ChatGPT. First major AI assistant with a commercially available ad product. Content optimized for ChatGPT citation now has a dual benefit: organic AI visibility + paid amplification.

3. **AI Overviews at 58% of searches:** Up from ~47% in early 2025. Pages with prior featured snippets get cited in AI Overviews at 2x the rate of non-snippet pages.

4. **Agentic AI becomes mainstream:** 2/3 of brand and agency buyers focused on agentic AI for ad buying. 80% of marketing processes now AI-automated or augmented (Gartner).

5. **Zero-click expands beyond Google:** Now spans ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Bing, and Meta AI. 60% of searches end without a click. Being THE cited source matters more than ranking #1.

### AEO Content Format Recommendations

Based on current AI citation patterns:
- **Answer-first paragraphs:** 40-60 words directly under H2/H3 headings
- **FAQ schema:** Cluster primary question + top 3 follow-ups
- **Comparison tables:** AI models extract structured data reliably
- **E-E-A-T signals:** Use "I/We", share case studies, name authors
- **Topic clusters > single pages:** AI models now cluster related questions

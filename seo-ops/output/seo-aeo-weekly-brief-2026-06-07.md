All 30 matches are false positives (whisky/food context). Despite ranking #1 in 20+ Google AI Overviews at the page level, the brand itself is not yet being recommended by name in AI answers. **Action needed:** Build brand-entity authority so AI models associate "Single Grain" with marketing expertise, not just cite individual pages. Recommended: create branded frameworks (e.g., "The Single Grain AEO Method"), publish original research, and build entity consistency across platforms.

## Scoring Methodology

**SEO Score (0-10):** Volume weight (40%) + Position gap opportunity (30%) + CPC/commercial value (20%) + Low difficulty bonus (10%)
**AEO Score (0-10):** AI Overview present in SERP (3pts) + Definitive-answer format fit (2pts) + Informational/how-to intent (2pts) + Featured snippet/question SERP features (2pts) + Citation potential (1pt)
**Combined Priority:** (SEO Score + AEO Score) / 2, weighted toward AEO for dual-opportunity keywords

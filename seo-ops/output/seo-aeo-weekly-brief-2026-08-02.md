## Industry Trend Signals (Week of 2026-08-02)

### Macro Trends Affecting SEO/AEO Strategy

1. **AI Overviews reducing organic CTR by 18-47%** — Google AI Overviews now appear on most informational queries. Being CITED in these overviews is the new #1 position.

2. **25% of search traffic shifting to AI chatbots** (Gartner) — ChatGPT has 800M weekly users. Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude are growing. AEO is no longer optional.

3. **EU AI Act high-risk provisions in effect Aug 2026** — Content about AI compliance, ethics, and governance is spiking. New content opportunity.

4. **Agentic AI adoption accelerating** — 40% of enterprise apps will have AI agents by end of 2026. 62% of orgs experimenting. Single Grain's "agentic AI marketing" content is well-positioned.

5. **Zero-click searches now ~70%** — Answers delivered directly through snippets, AI results, and voice. "Definitive answer" content format is critical.

6. **Programmatic SEO getting more sophisticated** — AI-powered content at scale but with mandatory human oversight. Sites using pure AI spinning got penalized.

## Methodology

- **SEO Score (0-100):** Based on search volume, current position proximity to page 1, keyword difficulty, CPC value, and existing content authority.
- **AEO Score (0-100):** Based on whether AI Overviews appear in SERPs, "definitive answer" potential (how-to, definition, comparison, list format), likelihood of AI model citation, and topic relevance to AI-generated responses.
- **Combined Priority:** Sum of SEO + AEO scores (max 200).
- **Data sources:** Ahrefs Site Explorer, Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, web trend research.
- **Note:** GSC direct API data was unavailable this cycle (no token configured). Striking-distance analysis used Ahrefs organic keyword positions as proxy.

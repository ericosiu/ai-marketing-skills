# Opportunity Routing

Record each atom's ID, timestamps, speaker, viewer, problem, promise, payoff, type, context, dependencies, claim and rights status, visual inputs, formats, platform, job, destination, scores, and verdict.

Score 0–5 for hook, completeness, novelty, proof, utility, visual potential, and strategic fit. Subtract 0–5 for claim uncertainty, rights risk, repetition, and repair effort. Treat the score as decision support.

Route dependent atoms to long-form; a sustained atom or cluster to mid-form; a complete 15–90 second atom to short-form; a 6–20 second hook to micro/promo; sequential or spatial value to a carousel; and citation-heavy or scannable value to written formats.

Use these verdicts:

- **Produce:** clears proof, completeness, strategic fit, and differentiation.
- **Repair:** needs a pickup, source, visual, bridge, or permission.
- **Defer:** viable but not useful in the current wave.
- **Reject:** generic, repetitive, unsupported, incomplete, unsafe, or off-strategy.

Record a reason for every verdict and recommend a bounded first release wave.

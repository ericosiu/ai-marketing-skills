# Quality Gates

## Universal editorial gate

- State viewer, verdict, proof, framework, and outcome.
- Give every asset one promise, job, payoff, and destination.
- Preserve context and never splice a claim the speaker did not make.
- Mark targets, projections, estimates, anecdotes, and tests visibly.
- Identify narration, pickups, synthetic media, and external research.
- Treat memo timestamps as guides, never literal cut points.

## Rights and claims

- Record provenance and permitted use for third-party media, logos, screenshots, music, and guest material.
- Keep achieved results distinct from targets, forecasts, estimates, and anecdotes.
- Do not imply causality from raw views or small samples.
- Flag unfamiliar names and numbers rather than guessing.
- Surface confidentiality, embargo, sponsor, disclosure, and consent issues.

## Media and editorial flow

- Default long/mid-form: H.264, AAC, yuv420p, progressive 16:9.
- Default short/micro: 1080×1920, H.264/AAC, 30 fps unless required otherwise.
- Full-stream decode reports zero errors and A/V durations differ by at most 100 ms.
- Dialogue target is approximately −16 LUFS stereo with true peak at or below −1 dBTP.
- Every retained clip begins and ends on complete thoughts.
- The pre-render boundary and post-render semantic-join audits pass.
- Decode, sync, loudness, and silence do not count as editorial-flow evidence.
- Repairs preserve previous versioned masters.

## Captions, overlays, packaging, and delivery

- Burned captions plus matching SRT are the default.
- SRT cues are monotonic, non-overlapping, at most two lines, mobile-safe, and end by the master.
- Use the brief’s opening treatment; a four-second overlay is a starting point when unspecified. Check the spoken hook independently.
- Captions move around lower thirds, graphics, and qualifiers; qualifiers take priority.
- Package intent is browse, search, or conversion and the opening pays it off.
- Variants test promise hypotheses, not colors.
- No external release action is claimed without approval and readback.
- Manifest modules match produced modules; files exist and hashes match.

## Source coverage and review readiness

- Inventory and synchronize all authorized camera, screen, slide, and playback-audio tracks.
- Map each spoken demonstration reference to visible, legible evidence. Missing proof is a repair, not a passing review candidate.
- Review the full output with audio and inspect joins, layout changes, proof, captions, and ending. Record exact version and reviewer evidence.
- Keep technical, editorial, caption, claim, rights, and approval results separate. Unknown or pending checks remain pending.
- Read [editorial-review.md](editorial-review.md) for the review record and [editing-workflow.md](editing-workflow.md) for preview and handoff acceptance.

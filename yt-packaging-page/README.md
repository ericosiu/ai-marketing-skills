# yt-packaging-page

Package a video before it goes out, long-form or short-form. One run produces titles, thumbnail text, a cut plan, Shorts and Reels hooks and captions, a chop plan, and a ranked list of what to make next, as one page.

## What it does

- Pulls real title data for reference channels and counts the patterns that win.
- Writes 5 titles per cut (60 characters or fewer), marks one REC, and cites the real video pattern it copies.
- Plans short-form: a hook in the first 1 to 2 seconds, 9:16 framing, captions, length, endings, and a chop plan with timestamps.
- Keeps failures in a separate fix list so video ideas lead with wins.
- Drafts only. It never uploads, publishes, schedules or retitles.

## Use it

Ask your agent: "Package this video, long-form and short-form, modeled on these channels." Give it the video, its chapters and one to three reference channels.

See [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) for the steps and rules. Pair it with `packaging-youtube-thumbnails` to render thumbnails and `shortform-production` or `video-clip-pipeline` to cut the clips.
